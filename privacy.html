In short: Bizora helps businesses create an online presence. We use information needed to operate the service, protect accounts, and display business details that users choose to publish. Please do not submit information you are not authorized to share.

1. Overview

Bizora ("Bizora", "we", "our", or "us") is a platform intended to help businesses establish and manage an online presence.

This Privacy Policy describes how information is handled when you visit our website, create an account, submit business information, or use related features.

By using Bizora, you acknowledge that you have read this policy. Where applicable law requires consent for a particular activity, we will seek that consent separately.

2. Information we collect

Account information

When you register, we may process your name, email address, account identifier, authentication details handled by our authentication provider, and information needed to maintain your account.

Business information

If you create a business profile, we may collect the business name, category, contact person's name, phone number, WhatsApp number if provided, address, description, logo, website link, social media links, and other information you submit.

Products and services

If catalogue features are available, we may process product or service names, descriptions, prices, images, and availability information you choose to add.

Technical information

Our hosting, authentication, or database providers may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser details, timestamps, and security logs as needed to deliver and protect the service. The information actually collected depends on the features and provider configuration in use.

Communications

If you contact us for support, we may retain the message and the contact information you provide so we can respond.

3. How we use information

We may use information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Save and maintain business profiles and catalogues.
  • Display business information that users choose to publish.
  • Provide account-related messages and support.
  • Maintain platform security, investigate misuse, and prevent fraud.
  • Diagnose errors and improve the platform.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations and protect legitimate rights.

We do not claim to use information for purposes that the platform does not actually perform. If new features introduce additional data uses, this policy may be updated.

4. Public business information

Bizora is designed to help businesses become discoverable. If you publish a business profile or catalogue, information designated as public may be visible to visitors and may be copied, indexed, or shared by third parties.

Before publishing, review your business name, address, contact details, images, product details, and links carefully. Do not publish private customer information or another person's details without appropriate permission.

Information saved as unpublished or private should not be treated as public. However, no online system can guarantee absolute security. Contact us if information appears publicly when it should not.

5. Sharing information

We may share limited information in these circumstances:

  • Service providers: Providers that help operate hosting, authentication, databases, file storage, or related infrastructure.
  • Public visitors: Business information you choose to publish.
  • Legal and safety reasons: Where disclosure is required by law or reasonably necessary to protect users, the platform, or others.
  • With your direction: When you ask us to share information or use a feature that makes it available.

We do not sell your personal information as a business model. We do not promise that every third-party service will be used in every situation; actual sharing depends on the features deployed and providers configured.

6. Data security

We use the security controls available in our infrastructure and application to help protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration.

No website, internet transmission, or storage system is completely secure. Keep your password private, use a strong password, and contact us if you suspect unauthorized access to your account.

7. Data retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain accounts and records, resolve disputes, enforce our terms, and comply with applicable law.

Deleting an account or requesting deletion may not immediately remove all copies from backups, security logs, or records that must be retained by law. Where applicable, we will handle deletion requests in accordance with legal requirements.

8. Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law and the features available, you may be able to:

  • Review and update your account or business information.
  • Unpublish or correct business information you control.
  • Request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information.
  • Withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.
  • Raise a concern about how your information is handled.

To make a privacy request, use the contact method provided in the Contact section. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

9. Cookies and local storage

Bizora or its service providers may use browser storage, cookies, or similar technologies where needed for functions such as authentication, session management, security, and remembering preferences.

The exact technologies depend on the features and integrations enabled. Blocking browser storage may affect some functionality. If analytics or advertising technologies are added later, this policy should be updated to describe them accurately.

10. Children's privacy

Bizora is intended for people who can legally use the service and manage the business information they submit. It is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has submitted personal information improperly, contact us.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our service, data practices, or legal obligations change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised date. Where required, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent.

12. Contact us

For privacy questions, correction requests, or deletion requests, contact the Bizora team through the official contact method provided on the Bizora website.
